# Origlyph AI — Customer Discovery Questions

**Purpose:** Understand the customer's current tolerance engineering process
**Use:** During or after the demo, or in a dedicated discovery session
**Format:** Select the most relevant questions — do not read all 28 aloud

---

## Tolerance Process

1. How are tolerance stacks currently calculated in your organisation?
2. Who performs the tolerance analysis — dedicated specialists, design engineers, or both?
3. How often is a tolerance stack re-calculated after a design change?
4. At what stage of the design process does tolerance analysis typically happen —
   concept, detailed design, design review, or post-release?
5. How long does a typical tolerance stack analysis take from start to reviewed result?

---

## Tools

6. What tools do you currently use — Excel, a CAD add-on, a specialist tool, or manual calculation?
7. Do different teams or projects use different tools or methods?
8. Is there a central template or standard approach, or does each engineer work independently?
9. How are analysis results stored and versioned today?

---

## Pain Points

10. What is the most time-consuming part of your current tolerance analysis workflow?
11. Have you experienced situations where a tolerance decision was questioned or could not be reproduced?
12. How difficult is it to communicate a tolerance decision and its rationale to reviewers or suppliers?
13. How much rework results from tolerance issues discovered late in the process?
14. Is there pressure to reduce tolerance analysis cycle time without reducing rigour?

---

## Standards and Rules

15. Which standards or OEM rules govern your tolerance specifications (e.g. ISO, ASME Y14.5,
    customer-specific requirements)?
16. Are tolerance limits defined in company standards, or are they set case-by-case?
17. Do you have specific statistical requirements (e.g. Cpk targets, sigma levels)?

---

## Data and Traceability

18. Where are current tolerance decisions and their supporting calculations documented?
19. Is there a formal approval process for tolerance decisions, and how is it recorded?
20. How often are historical tolerance decisions reviewed or audited?
21. Can you currently trace a PASS/FAIL decision back to the specific inputs and
    calculation version that produced it?

---

## Decision Process

22. Who has authority to approve a tolerance decision — the design engineer, a specialist,
    a quality function, or a committee?
23. How is a tolerance decision communicated to manufacturing or the supply chain?
24. How are tolerance exceptions or waivers handled and documented?

---

## Supplier Collaboration

25. Do you share tolerance stack analyses with suppliers? If so, how?
26. Have supplier tolerance contributions ever caused a tolerance stack to fail?

---

## Reporting and Integration

27. Do you currently generate tolerance reports for customer or internal review?
    What format do they take?
28. Are there systems (PLM, PDM, ERP) you would expect a tolerance tool to integrate with?

---

## Pilot Opportunity

29. Is there a current project or assembly where a structured tolerance analysis
    would add value? Could that be a starting point for an evaluation?
30. What would a successful pilot look like from your perspective —
    what would it need to demonstrate or deliver?
