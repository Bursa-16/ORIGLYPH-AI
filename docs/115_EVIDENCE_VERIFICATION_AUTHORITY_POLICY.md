## 2. Purpose

This policy defines when evidence may become authoritative and identifies the
separate approvals required before governed engineering or runtime actions may
proceed. It establishes fail-closed boundaries between observation, human
verification, promotion, authoritative declaration, interpretation, and
implementation.

## 3. Scope

This policy covers:

- drawing-derived evidence;
- OCR, parser, PMI, AI, image-analysis, and automated-inference outputs;
- human transcription;
- evidence verification and promotion;
- reviewer governance;
- approval evidence; and
- implementation and migration authority boundaries.

This policy does not define:

- standards rule content;
- OEM rule content;
- runtime implementation details;
- cryptographic-signature design; or
- RBAC implementation.

## 4. Authority Model

The evidence authority chain is:

```text
OBSERVATION
-> HUMAN VERIFICATION
-> PROMOTION
-> AUTHORITATIVE DECLARATION
```

Engineering interpretation is a separate downstream chain:

```text
AUTHORITATIVE DECLARATION
-> RESOLUTION
-> CAD BINDING
-> ROLE ASSIGNMENT
-> DRF / ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION
```

No stage may silently skip the preceding authority boundary. Verification or
promotion must not silently bind CAD geometry, assign `ConstraintType`, assign
`PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, or `TERTIARY`, construct a `DatumConstraint`, construct
a `DatumReferenceFrame`, select a governing profile, or validate proprietary
standards content.

## 5. Evidence Classes

These terms are policy terminology and do not define implementation enums:

- `MACHINE_OBSERVATION`: information extracted, inferred, classified, or
  generated by a machine process without human verification.
- `HUMAN_TRANSCRIPTION`: source content entered by an identifiable human
  without an implied interpretive or approval decision.
- `VERIFIED_EVIDENCE`: evidence explicitly checked by an attributed human
  under applicable governance.
- `AUTHORITATIVE_DECLARATION`: a promoted governed record that has satisfied
  its required authority boundary.
- `ASSOCIATION_EVIDENCE`: evidence supporting a relationship between source
  facts or governed objects without silently creating that relationship.
- `REVIEW_EVIDENCE`: durable evidence of the content and basis reviewed by a
  qualified reviewer.
- `APPROVAL_EVIDENCE`: durable evidence of an attributed approval decision
  tied to exact reviewable content.

## 6. Machine Evidence Firewall

Machine-derived evidence may:

- extract;
- classify;
- suggest;
- rank items for human review; and
- surface provenance and supporting evidence.

Machine-derived evidence may not:

- promote itself;
- authorize declarations;
- select a governing profile;
- approve reviewer independence;
- bind evidence to CAD;
- assign engineering roles;
- validate standards content; or
- grant implementation or migration authority.

OCR, PMI extraction, parsers, AI, image analysis, and automated inference
remain non-authoritative until explicit human verification and promotion. AI
is advisory only for governed decisions. External or public AI/CDN use remains
prohibited wherever DEC-010 applies.

## 7. Human Verification

Human verification is an attributed action. At minimum, its record must make
available, where governed and applicable:

- the identifiable human actor;
- the exact source inspected;
- the source reference or revision where available;
- the observed content;
- the accepted content when it differs from the observation; and
- the explicit decision.

Verification must not silently resolve ambiguity. Missing or ambiguous
authority, source, association, or decision evidence fails closed. This policy
does not require unapproved fields or invent a verification implementation.

## 8. Promotion

Promotion is explicit and attributable. It creates a new authoritative record
or decision artifact and does not mutate the source evidence. A promotion must
identify the evidence and authority on which it relies. Machine output and
unverified transcription cannot promote themselves.

## 9. Reviewer Independence and Competence

Reviewer qualification, independence, sign-off, and release validation are
governed by DEC-005. Independence cannot be reduced to a simple comparison such
as `reviewer != transcriber`; it requires the approved governance context.

Competence is a governance and qualification concern, not a drawing or domain
fact. Reviewer approval must not be embedded as drawing/domain truth. Final
independence and qualification rules remain pending until DEC-005 is approved.

## 10. Standards and OEM Firewalls

- DEC-001 gates standards-interpretive behavior.
- DEC-002 gates licensed standards content.
- DEC-003 separately gates OEM-controlled content.

Approval of SDS-115 cannot override any of these gates. Exact-string storage
or matching and neutral source-fact capture are permitted when they perform no
standards interpretation. Proprietary standards content and OEM-controlled
content require their own applicable authority.

## 11. Approval Roles

The following grants are distinct:

- Architecture approval;
- Security approval;
- Data Owner approval;
- Verification Authority;
- Implementation Authority; and
- Migration Authority.

Each grant remains independent unless another approved governance artifact
explicitly links it. Silence, role title, administrative access, or possession
of a document does not create a grant.

## 12. Architecture Approval Boundary

Architecture approval may confirm:

- structure;
- dependency direction;
- authority boundaries;
- fail-closed design; and
- policy consistency.

Architecture approval does not grant Security approval, Data Owner approval,
Verification Authority, Implementation Authority, or Migration 0006 authority.

## 13. Security Approval Boundary

Security approval governs policy-level security, privacy, and processing
controls. This document does not identify or fabricate a Security authority
holder, and it does not grant Security approval.

## 14. Data Owner Approval Boundary

Data Owner approval governs the use of controlled, proprietary, OEM, and
customer data. This document does not identify or fabricate a Data Owner
authority holder, and it does not grant Data Owner approval.

## 15. Verification Authority Boundary

Verification Authority determines which human actors and governed processes
may issue authoritative verification. SDS-115 does not grant Verification
Authority.

## 16. Implementation Authority Boundary

Implementation requires an explicit separate authorization after all required
prerequisite approvals are satisfied. Document approval alone does not unlock
implementation.

```text
IMPLEMENTATION_UNLOCKED = NO
```

## 17. Migration 0006 Boundary

```text
MIGRATION_0006_ALLOWED = NO
```

Migration 0006 remains prohibited unless a separate authorized decision

## 18. Fail-Closed Rules

- Missing authority blocks the governed action.
- Ambiguous authority blocks the governed action.
- Ambiguous source association blocks promotion and downstream use.
- Unverifiable review content prevents approval.
- A non-resolving review reference prevents approval.
- Machine-only evidence is not authoritative.
- An unknown governing profile blocks standards interpretation.
- Missing licensed-content authority blocks standards-rule use.
- Missing OEM permission blocks OEM-controlled content use.
- Missing or ambiguous required evidence never defaults to approval.

## 19. Approval Evidence Integrity

A durable approval evidence reference may use:

```text
repository-relative path + SHA-256 of exact final artifact bytes
```

SHA-256 provides content integrity only. It is not a cryptographic human
signature and does not prove human identity by itself. Approval evidence must
also contain explicit human attestation and a durable human identity.

## 20. Approval Record Requirements

A future approval record must identify:

- `DOCUMENT_ID`;
- `DOCUMENT_VERSION`;
- `REVIEW_CONTENT_REFERENCE`;
- `APPROVAL_ROLE`;
- `APPROVER_DURABLE_HUMAN_IDENTITY`;
- `ROLE_AND_AUTHORITY`;
- `DECISION`;
- UTC decision timestamp;
- `APPROVER_ATTESTATION`; and
- durable evidence reference.

The record must also state explicit non-grants for every authority not included
in the decision.

## 21. Change Control

Any substantive modification to SDS-115 after approval invalidates the prior
content-specific approval for the new bytes or version. The changed content
requires a new review reference and new human attestation. Approval must not be
silently carried forward across changed content.

Immutable evidence records are preferred over mutation of frozen declarations.
