# Origlyph Clean-Room Policy

This policy governs how source and reference material may be used in Origlyph.

## Prohibited Material

* No migration of legacy DatumIQ source.
* No copying of TorqPro source or material.
* No copying of SpotWeld-AI source or material.
* No use of OEM/FCA proprietary material.
* No inclusion of proprietary standards text or tables unless independently
  authorized.

## Allowed Practice

* Reference material may guide clean-room reimplementation.

## Provenance

* Engineering logic must have traceable provenance or be derived from
  first-principles derivation.
